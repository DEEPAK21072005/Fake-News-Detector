# Future Research Directions

## 1. Graph Neural Networks for Narrative Propagation
Future extensions will incorporate Graph Neural Networks (GNNs) to model the propagation graphs of claims across social networks, capturing user interaction dynamics and echo-chamber clustering.

## 2. Temporal Knowledge Graphs
Integrating dynamic, temporal knowledge graphs (TKGs) will enable the system to model evolving entity relations, ensuring that past historical facts are not erroneously applied to current dynamic events.

## 3. Multimodal Cross-Attention Deep Alignment
Developing lightweight cross-attention heads that compute fine-grained token-to-patch bounding box alignment between article claims and image regions, operating efficiently on mobile and edge CPUs.

## 4. Decentralized Evidence Consensus
Federating the evidence retrieval layer across distributed, cryptographic fact-checking registries to eliminate single-point-of-failure bias in evidence databases.
